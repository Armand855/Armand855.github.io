No-Bake Cookies

This recpie for no-bake cookies came into my family around 1950s by my great grandmother. "I first tried it in the 1980s. They were a special treat I looked forward to and I loved the cocoa and peanut butter combination!" My Mom said. I've selected this recipe because it always brings me joy when I hear that we're making no-bake cookies!

Tips, tricks and substitutions

  • You can make the recipe without the cocoa and just have oat and peanut butter no-bake cookies.
  • You can use regular butter instead of Margerine
  • Put a layer of parchment paper at the bottom so the cookies can come out easier.

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Sugar 4 cups
Cocoa powder 4 tablespoons
Milk 1 cup
Margerine 2 sticks
Oats 6 cups
Peanut butter 1 cup
Vanilla 1 teaspoon

Step by step

  1. Take out a large pot and put it on the stove. Line a 9x13-inch baking dish with tin foil.
  2. Gather your ingredients: sugar, cocoa powder, margerine, milk, oats, peanut butter and vanilla.
  3. Mix sugar, cocoa powder, margerine and milk in the pot. Bring to boil and allow to boil for 2 minutes. Stir frequently.
  4. Mix oats, peanut butter and vanilla together in a separate bowl.
  5. After boiling for 2 minutes, stir in the oats, peanut butter and vanilla.
  6. Pour the mix into the baking dish then place in the refridgerator for about 5 hours or until set.
  7. Enjoy your no-bake cookies!

    8. Serving size:1 square(24 servings in total)

    Preparation time:6 hours
