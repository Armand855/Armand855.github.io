No-Bake Cookies

ingredients

  • 4 cups of sugar
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 4 tablespoons of cocoa
  • 2 sticks Margerine
  • 6 cups of Oats
  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1 teaspoon Vannila

Step by step

  1. Put 4 Cups of sugar, 4 tablespoons of cocoa, 1 cup of milk, and 2 sticks of Margerine in a pot.
  2. Put the pot on the stove and bring it to a boil
  3. While the ingredients in the pot boil on the stove, mix 6 cups of oats, 1 cup of peanut butter, and 1 teaspoon of vanilla in a bowl.
  4. Mix the ingredints in the bowl and pour them into the pot
  5. Mix all the ingredients in the pot for 5-10 minutes.
  6. After mixing all the imgredients in the pot, pour it into a square glass container and let it sit in the fridge for 4-5 hours.
  7. Enjoy your no-bake cookies!
